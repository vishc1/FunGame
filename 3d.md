# 3D brief for Astra — Meme Karts

Paste everything below the line into Astra. It is written to be self-contained.

---

## The job

You are making the 3D art for **Meme Karts**, a browser kart-battle game in the shape of Smash Karts: small arena, chase camera, item boxes, silly weapons. The goal is "a Smash Karts replica, but more fun and with better graphics."

The game already runs in 3D with placeholder models built from boxes and cylinders in code. Your job is to replace those placeholders with proper models made in **Blender**, exported as **.glb** files. You do not need to write any game code. The game auto-detects files by name and swaps them in.

- Project folder: `~/friendgame`
- Put every export in: `~/friendgame/public/models/`
- Renderer (read-only reference, do not need to edit): `public/games/memekarts.js`
- Engine: Three.js r186, `MeshStandardMaterial`, ACES tone mapping, one sun with soft shadows, hemisphere sky light.

## Art direction

- **Chunky, toy-like low-poly** with soft bevels. Think Smash Karts / Mario Kart toys / Crossy Road, not realism.
- Saturated colours, clean shapes that read from 10 metres behind and above. Exaggerate proportions: big wheels, big heads, fat tyres.
- Flat or lightly graded colours. Use plain Principled BSDF materials (base colour, roughness, metallic, emission). **No image textures are required**; if you use them keep each at 512×512 or smaller and pack them into the .glb.
- Every item must be recognisable in under half a second as a silhouette. If two items could be confused (green shell vs red shell), make the colour and one shape detail obviously different.
- **Original parody designs only.** These are jokes that reference kart games and other things the friend group plays. Do not copy any real character, logo or exact trademarked design. "A grumpy round bomb with feet" is right; a replica of a specific Nintendo model is wrong.

## Hard rules for every model

1. **Forward is Blender +X. Up is Blender +Z.** The front of a kart, the nose of a shell, the pointy end of the bullet all face +X.
2. **Sits on the ground at Z = 0**, centred on the origin in X and Y.
3. **Apply all transforms** (Ctrl+A, All Transforms) and delete cameras, lights and empties before exporting.
4. **Scale does not matter.** The game measures each model and rescales it so its longest horizontal side matches the size in the table. Just keep proportions right. Model at a comfortable size, for example a kart about 3.6 m long.
5. **Triangle budgets** are in the table. Eight karts are on screen at once, plus up to ~40 items.
6. **Export:** File, Export, glTF 2.0. Format **glTF Binary (.glb)**. Tick **+Y Up**, **Apply Modifiers**. Include Selected Objects only. No animations, no cameras, no lights.
7. **File names must match the table exactly**, all lower-case.

### Extra rules for karts

- One material must be named exactly **`Paint`**. The game recolours that material to the player's colour (red, blue, green, yellow, purple, orange, teal, pink), so use it for the main bodywork and make sure the kart looks good in all eight. Model it in a neutral light grey.
- The four wheels must be **separate objects** named `wheel_fl`, `wheel_fr`, `wheel_rl`, `wheel_rr`, each with its **origin at the wheel centre** and its **axle along Blender Y**. The game spins them.
- Include a driver, visible from behind and above. The driver is the joke, so give each one a personality. Head and helmet should poke clearly above the bodywork.
- Collision is a circle of radius 1.6 m, so keep the footprint roughly 3.6 m long by 2.2 m wide, and under 3 m tall (the bus balloon may go higher).

### Added later: height (decks and ramps)

The maps now have real height. **Decks** are raised blocks 4.8 m tall that karts drive on top of. **Ramps** are 10 m long, 8.4 m wide slopes from the floor up to a deck. Every map also has one **sky tower**: a deck with a second deck stacked on it (10 m above the floor) and a second ramp on the first deck that climbs the last 5.2 m; the summit holds an item box and the drop off its edge is 10 m. Karts drive up a ramp, across the deck, and back down a ramp or straight off the edge. The game draws plain blocks and uses the existing `ramp.glb`; these optional models replace them:

| File | What it is | Shape | Max tris |
|---|---|---|---|
| `deck.glb` | Sunset Stadium deck: a chunky raised platform, e.g. a concrete podium with a hazard-striped skirt | cuboid, about 20 × 16 m, 4.8 m tall | 600 |
| `deck_neon.glb` | Neon Overdrive rooftop: a dark building block with lit windows on the sides and a helipad-style roof | cuboid, about 30 × 22 m, 4.8 m tall | 800 |
| `deck_canyon.glb` | Canyon Rush plateau: a flat-topped sandstone mesa with layered sides | cuboid, about 22 × 12 m, 4.8 m tall | 600 |
| `ramp_neon.glb`, `ramp_canyon.glb` | themed versions of `ramp.glb` (same rules) | wedge rising toward +X | 400 each |

Tier-two decks (the tower summits) reuse the same deck model, stretched from the floor to 10 m, and sit entirely on top of a first deck, so their lower half is hidden inside it. Optional themed summit models: `tower.glb`, `tower_neon.glb`, `tower_canyon.glb`, same rules as decks (filled bounding box, flat level top), e.g. a radio mast podium, a neon penthouse, a sandstone pinnacle; the game stretches them to the tier-two footprint and to 10 m.

Rules for decks: the **top must be flat and level** (karts drive on it) and the model must **fill its whole bounding box footprint** (the collision is the exact rectangle). It is stretched to each deck's footprint and to 3.2 m tall, so keep detail that survives stretching, and keep the top surface readable from the chase camera: karts need to see the edge. The game adds its own glowing rim around the top edge and leaves gaps where ramps join, so do not model railings. Ramps should be a loud colour with arrows pointing uphill; a dark ramp reads as a floor marking.

### `ramp.glb`

The maps now have jump ramps. The game draws a plain orange wedge with chevrons. Optional model `ramp.glb`: a kicker ramp that **rises toward Blender +X** (low edge at −X, high edge at +X), about 7.2 m long, 6.4 m wide and 1.5 m high at the lip, max 400 triangles. Make the sides solid, paint arrows up the slope, and give the lip a bright edge. It is stretched to each ramp's exact footprint and rotated to face its direction, so keep it a simple wedge.

### Optional big one: `arena.glb`

If this file exists the game hides its placeholder floor, walls, obstacles and scenery and shows yours instead. Collision is fixed on the server, so the layout must match exactly:

- Game east is Blender +X. Game north (top of the minimap) is Blender **+Y**.
- **Drivable floor:** rectangle X from −43 to +43, Y from −28 to +28.
- **Perimeter wall** just outside that rectangle, 2 to 2.5 m tall.
- **Seven obstacles**, each about 3.3 m tall (centre X, centre Y, size X × size Y):

## How to check your work

```bash
cd ~/friendgame && npm start
```

1. Open http://localhost:3000 and click **🧪 Test mode** in the bottom-right corner. Three bots join.
2. Pick **Meme Karts**, press Start. W/A/S/D drives, Space fires.
3. http://localhost:3000/models/list shows which files the game found. Anything missing falls back to its placeholder, so you can deliver in any order and test as you go.

Acceptance checklist for each model:

- Faces the right way when driving forward, and sits on the floor (not sunk in, not floating).
- Reads clearly from the chase camera at speed.
- Karts: the `Paint` material changes with the player colour, and all four wheels spin.
- No black or pink surfaces (missing or unsupported materials), no flipped normals.

## Stretch goals, only if asked to also work on code

Stay inside `public/games/memekarts.js`. Do not change the server or the message format.

- Play animations baked into the .glb files (the loader currently ignores them).
- Skid marks on the floor, dust when landing, tyre smoke on spin-outs.
- Bloom on emissive materials and a subtle vignette.
- Sound: engine pitch from speed, item pickup, hits, explosions.
